The Ship's Articles

At the Helm

  • W/S make / shorten sail
  • A/D helm to larboard / starboard
  • Mouse look around · Wheel zoom
  • Left Click fire the broadside facing your view
  • Q/E fire larboard / starboard guns
  • Right Click spyglass (hold) · raise to aim higher
  • 1 round · 2 chain · 3 grape shot
  • F board a struck prize · go ashore · dock
  • G hoist the Jolly Roger / false colours
  • R drop / weigh anchor

Ashore

  • WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump
  • Left Click cutlass (click again to combo)
  • Right Click aim flintlock · Left Click fire
  • E enter shops · talk · dig · loot
  • F return to your ship

Anywhere

  • M sea chart (fast travel to known ports)
  • Tab captain's log
  • Esc pause

Every captain signs the articles. Notoriety rises when you attack a nation's ships or subjects — at two skulls their navy hunts you, at four their pirate-hunters never give up. Lie low, pay off governors, or take pardons through contracts to cool your name.
